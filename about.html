About

A watchmaker’s eye. A photographer’s vision.

My path in watch photography began at the bench: for two years I repaired and assembled wristwatches. That hands-on experience taught me to understand watchmaking from the inside and to value the details that set truly exceptional timepieces apart.

Today I bring what landscape and architectural photography taught me — light, colour and composition — to my still-life work, revealing the precision, craftsmanship and character of every piece.
