About

Before photography, I spent years as a watchmaker. I know a movement from the inside, how it is finished and how it is assembled, and I photograph timepieces with that knowledge. It shows in every frame.

Landscape photography has been my parallel discipline for just as long. It taught me to work with light, scale and atmosphere, and it is what brings beauty, not only precision, into my product images.

Commissioned product work is my profession, and every year I still spend a great deal of time in the field. I am a member of the WPE and 35PHOTO.PRO communities, and a laureate and prize-winner of international competitions in landscape, architectural and aerial photography.

I work worldwide, in my studio or on location, and deliver retouched files ready for print, web and campaigns.
